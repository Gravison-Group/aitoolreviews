Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 2026  ·  AIToolReviews.co

This Privacy Policy explains how AIToolReviews.co ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our site. We keep it straightforward — no legalese walls.

What We Collect

Email address — only if you voluntarily submit a contact or signup form. We use Formspree to process form submissions. Your email is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Usage data — we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, time on site, general location, device type). This data is aggregated and anonymous from our perspective.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to distinguish visitors and track sessions. These cookies do not personally identify you. You can opt out by:

  • Installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Using your browser's built-in privacy/cookie controls
  • Enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser settings

Affiliate Links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate partners may use their own cookies and tracking technologies. We only recommend products we believe are genuinely useful.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics — Google Privacy Policy
  • Formspree — Formspree Privacy Policy

Data Retention

We do not store personal data beyond what Formspree retains for form submissions (typically 30–90 days for free plans). Analytics data is retained per Google's standard retention settings.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of any personal data we may hold by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

Changes
