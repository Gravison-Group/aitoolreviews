What is Google Gemini?

Google Gemini is Google's flagship AI assistant and model family, developed by Google DeepMind. It was launched as Bard in 2023 and rebranded to Gemini in February 2024. The Gemini model family includes three tiers: Nano (on-device), Flash (fast, efficient), and Pro (most capable).

Gemini 1.5 Pro is notable for its extraordinary 1 million token context window — about 8x larger than GPT-4o's 128K. This means you can feed it entire codebases, legal documents, or lengthy research reports in a single prompt. It's natively multimodal, meaning it can understand text, images, audio, and video inputs simultaneously.

Performance & Capabilities

Context Window (1M Tokens)

Gemini 1.5 Pro's 1 million token context window is its defining feature. We tested it with a 500-page legal document and a 20,000-line codebase — it handled both without losing track of earlier content. For legal professionals, researchers, and developers working with large bodies of text, this alone makes Gemini worth evaluating.

Google Workspace Integration

Gemini Advanced is available directly inside Gmail, Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides. You can ask Gemini to summarize email threads, draft replies, analyze spreadsheet data, or create presentation slides — all without leaving the Google app. This level of integration is unmatched by ChatGPT or Claude for Google-native users.

Real-Time Search

Unlike ChatGPT (where web search requires Plus), Gemini includes Google Search integration on the free tier. It can surface current information with linked sources, making it genuinely useful for research tasks without a paid subscription.

Multimodal Capabilities

Gemini natively understands images, audio, and video — not just text. You can upload a video and ask questions about its content, or provide an audio recording for transcription and analysis. This is more advanced than most competitors.

Our Verdict
Gemini 1.5 Pro earns a 4.3/5 — solid but not leading on general AI tasks. If you're a Google Workspace power user or need to process extremely large documents, Gemini is the right choice. For general writing, coding, or research without a Google dependency, ChatGPT or Claude remain stronger picks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Google Gemini free?
Yes. Gemini has a free tier using Gemini 1.5 Flash. Gemini Advanced ($20/month via Google One AI Premium) gives you Gemini 1.5 Pro, longer conversations, and full Workspace integration.
What is Gemini 1.5 Pro's context window?
1 million tokens — the largest publicly available context window as of 2024. That's roughly 750,000 words or 1,500 pages of text.
How does Gemini compare to ChatGPT?
Gemini trails ChatGPT on general reasoning and writing. However, it has a larger context window and better Google Workspace integration. Choose Gemini if you live in Google apps; choose ChatGPT for general-purpose AI use.
Can Gemini access real-time information?
Yes. Gemini includes Google Search on the free tier, giving it access to current information without needing a paid subscription.
What is the difference between Gemini and Bard?
Bard was rebranded to Gemini in February 2024. Gemini uses more advanced models and has significantly improved capabilities. Bard no longer exists as a separate product.
