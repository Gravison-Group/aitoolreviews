What is Perplexity AI?

Perplexity AI is an AI-powered search engine launched in 2022 by Aravind Srinivas and a team of ex-Google and ex-OpenAI researchers. Unlike traditional search engines (which return ranked links) or standalone AI chatbots (which work from training data alone), Perplexity combines both: it searches the web in real time, then uses an LLM to synthesize a direct, cited answer.

The result is something genuinely new. Ask Perplexity "What's the current status of OpenAI's board situation?" and you'll get a concise summary with six numbered citations you can click to verify. Ask ChatGPT the same question and you might get outdated information presented with equal confidence.

Performance & Capabilities

Real-Time Search & Citations

This is Perplexity's defining strength. Every response is grounded in current web results, and every factual claim gets a numbered superscript citation linking to the original source. In our testing, Perplexity's answers on recent events were consistently accurate and up-to-date — something impossible for knowledge-cutoff-limited models like baseline ChatGPT.

Answer Quality

Perplexity's answers are concise and well-organized. It avoids the verbose padding you sometimes get from ChatGPT and Claude. For factual queries, this is a feature — you get the answer, not an essay. For creative tasks or complex analysis, this brevity can feel limiting.

Focus Modes

Perplexity offers focus modes that restrict search to specific sources: Academic (peer-reviewed papers), YouTube (video summaries), Reddit (community discussions), and Social. These are genuinely useful — asking Perplexity to find Reddit discussions about a product gives you real user opinions, not SEO-optimized reviews.

Pro Features

Perplexity Pro lets you choose between GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro, and Perplexity's own Sonar models. You essentially get a premium subscription to multiple AI models through one interface. For the research-focused user, this is excellent value.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Perplexity AI?
Perplexity AI is an AI-powered search engine that answers questions using real-time web search. It synthesizes information from multiple sources into a direct answer with numbered citations.
Is Perplexity AI free?
Yes. The free tier includes unlimited searches, real-time web access, and source citations. Pro ($20/month) adds access to GPT-4o, Claude, and Gemini, plus file uploads and more Pro searches per day.
How accurate is Perplexity AI?
More accurate than standalone AI chatbots for current-events questions. It can occasionally misinterpret sources, so always verify important facts against the cited links provided.
Can Perplexity replace Google Search?
For research and complex information queries, often yes. Google remains better for local search, shopping, and maps. Many users find themselves using both tools.
