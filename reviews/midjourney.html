What is Midjourney?

Midjourney is an AI image generator that creates images from text descriptions (prompts). It was launched in July 2022 by Midjourney Inc. and quickly gained a reputation for producing some of the most visually stunning AI-generated images available. Unlike text-based AI tools, Midjourney specializes entirely in image creation.

Midjourney v6, released in late 2023 and updated through 2026, represents a major leap in photorealism and prompt accuracy. It can generate detailed portraits, landscapes, product shots, abstract art, and virtually any visual style you can describe.

Performance & Image Quality

v6 Photorealism

Midjourney v6's photorealistic output is genuinely impressive. Portrait shots in particular are indistinguishable from photography to the untrained eye. Lighting, skin texture, and environmental detail are rendered with a level of consistency that's still ahead of DALL-E 3 and most Stable Diffusion checkpoints.

Artistic Styles

Beyond photorealism, Midjourney excels at distinctive artistic styles — oil painting, watercolor, concept art, anime, architectural visualization, product design. The model has a clear aesthetic sensibility: images tend to be composed beautifully, with strong contrast and color use. Many designers use this "Midjourney look" intentionally.

Text in Images

A historic weakness of AI image generators — rendering readable text — is dramatically improved in v6. Short phrases and signs are now mostly legible, though longer text still struggles. It's not perfect, but it's good enough for many use cases where v5 failed entirely.

Midjourney vs DALL-E 3

DALL-E 3 (built into ChatGPT Plus) is more convenient — you describe what you want in natural language and get a good result. Midjourney requires more deliberate prompting but rewards it with higher-quality outputs. For professional and commercial work, Midjourney wins. For quick, casual image generation, DALL-E 3's accessibility is hard to beat.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Midjourney the best AI image generator?
Midjourney v6 consistently produces the highest-quality AI images in 2026, particularly for photorealistic and artistic styles. DALL-E 3 is more convenient but lower quality. Stable Diffusion is free and customizable. For raw image quality, Midjourney is the benchmark.
How much does Midjourney cost?
Plans start at $10/month (Basic: ~200 images), $30/month (Standard: ~900 images + 15h fast), $60/month (Pro: private + 30h fast). Annual billing saves ~20%. No free tier currently.
Does Midjourney require Discord?
No longer. Midjourney launched a web interface at midjourney.com in 2026. Discord access is still available but not required for subscribers.
What is the difference between Midjourney v5 and v6?
v6 offers significantly better photorealism, improved text rendering in images, more accurate prompt adherence, and better fine detail. Most users should default to v6.
Can Midjourney be used commercially?
Yes, on all paid plans. Basic through Mega plans include commercial use rights. Always verify the current Terms of Service for details.
