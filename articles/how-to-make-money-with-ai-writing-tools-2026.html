7 Ways to Make Money with AI Writing Tools

1. Affiliate Marketing (Easiest to Start)

How it works: Recommend AI tools you use and earn commissions on sales.

  • Commission rates: 25-45% recurring (Copy.ai: 45% first month, Writesonic: 30% recurring)

2. Freelance AI Writing Services

How it works: Offer AI-assisted writing services on platforms like Upwork, Fiverr.

  • Services: Blog posts, product descriptions, email sequences, social media content
  • Pricing: $0.10-$0.30/word, or $50-$500/article
  • Tools needed: ChatGPT Plus, Jasper, or Writesonic

3. Content Agency Model

How it works: Build a team (or go solo) delivering high-volume content.

  • Client types: SaaS companies, e-commerce brands, agencies
  • Pricing: $1,000-$10,000/month retainer
  • Scale: 1 person + AI can deliver 50+ articles/month

4. Niche Site Building

How it works: Build authority sites in specific niches using AI-generated content.

  • Examples: Pet care (BusyPetParent.com), AI tools (AIToolReviews.co)
  • Revenue streams: Display ads, affiliate links, sponsored content
  • Time to revenue: 3-6 months for first income
