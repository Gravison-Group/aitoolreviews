FAQs

How accurate is Frase's AI writing?

Frase's AI writing is functional but often requires editing. It excels at research and structure but can produce generic or repetitive content. Most users treat it as a starting point rather than final output. The real value is in the research, optimization, and tracking features.

Is Frase's AI visibility tracking worth it?

Absolutely — this is Frase's unique advantage in 2026. As AI search platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) capture more queries, tracking content performance across these platforms becomes essential. Frase monitors 8 AI platforms, giving you insights traditional SEO tools miss.

Can Frase replace Surfer SEO or Clearscope?

For most teams, yes. Frase offers 80-90% of Surfer's optimization features plus AI writing and AI tracking. Clearscope has a more polished grading system, but Frase provides better overall value. Start with Frase's 7-day trial to see if it meets your needs.

How does Frase handle brand voice?

Well, but not exceptionally. Frase's brand voice profiles let you define tone, audience, and messaging guidelines. It's more functional than sophisticated — good enough for maintaining consistency but not as advanced as Jasper's brand voice training.

Is there a free plan?

No permanent free plan, but there's a 7-day free trial with full feature access (no credit card required). This is more generous than Surfer SEO's limited free trial but less than tools like Rytr that offer ongoing free tiers.

How does Frase's MCP server work?

Frase includes a read-write MCP (Model Context Protocol) server that lets AI agents like OpenClaw or Cursor access Frase's features programmatically. This means your AI agent can research keywords, generate content briefs, or run site audits without manual intervention — a powerful automation feature.
