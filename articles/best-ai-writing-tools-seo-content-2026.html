Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need both an AI writer and an SEO tool?

A: Ideally yes — an AI writer drafts content faster, while an SEO tool (Surfer, Frase) optimizes it for ranking. Writesonic integrates both in one platform, making it the most efficient single-tool solution.

Q: How many times should I use my keyword in an AI-written article?

A: Target keyword density of 0.5–1.5%. More important than density is using the keyword in the title, H1, first paragraph, and at least two subheadings. AI SEO tools like Surfer provide exact counts for your specific article.

Q: Can AI write content that ranks on the first page?

A: Yes — properly optimized AI content regularly ranks on page one, especially for long-tail keywords with moderate competition. The key is combining AI drafting with human editing and solid technical SEO.

Q: What's the fastest workflow for AI SEO content?

A: The fastest workflow: keyword → Frase brief (10 min) → Writesonic draft (5 min) → Surfer optimization (10 min) → human edit (15–20 min) → publish. A well-optimized 1,500-word article in under an hour.
