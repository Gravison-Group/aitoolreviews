Detailed Breakdown for Small Teams

Expensify: Best Overall for Small Teams

Why it works for small teams: At $5/user/month, Expensify provides enterprise-level prevention features at startup prices. For a 5-person team, that's just $25/month.

Small team advantages:

  • No minimum user count - Pay only for who uses it
  • Easy setup - Get running in under an hour
  • Policy blocking - Prevents the incomplete submissions that waste your time
  • SmartScan OCR - Reads receipts automatically (saves 2-3 minutes per receipt)

Cost for 5 users: $25/month ($300/year)

Best for: Small teams that need strong policy enforcement without complexity.

Zoho Expense: Most Budget-Friendly

Why it works for small teams: At $3/user/month, Zoho Expense is the most affordable quality option. For 5 users, that's just $15/month.

Small team advantages:

  • Lowest price of any quality expense software
  • Real-time policy checks - Flags violations as employees type
  • Multi-currency support - Good for teams with international expenses
  • Simple interface - Easy for non-finance people to use

Cost for 5 users: $15/month ($180/year)

Best for: Bootstrapped startups and teams on a tight budget.

QuickBooks Online: Best if You Already Use QuickBooks

Why it works for small teams: If you're already paying for QuickBooks Online ($30/month), adding expense tracking is $10/user/month.

Small team advantages:

  • No integration needed - Expenses flow directly to accounting
  • Mobile receipt capture with quality checking
  • Automatic matching to bank transactions
  • Mileage tracking - Auto-generates travel receipts

Cost for 5 users: $80/month ($30 base + 5×$10 = $960/year)

Best for: Small businesses already using QuickBooks for accounting.

Rydoo: Best for International Small Teams

Why it works for small teams: Specialized for international expenses at $7/user/month.

Small team advantages:

  • Multi-currency validation with real-time rates
  • VAT automation for EU compliance
  • Country-specific policies
  • Receipt translation for foreign language receipts

Cost for 5 users: $35/month ($420/year)

Best for: Small teams with international travel or remote employees in different countries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do we really need expense software with only 5 employees?

A: Yes. The time wasted chasing errors scales with team size. A 5-person team can waste 10-15 hours monthly—that's 2-3 days of lost productivity.

Q: What's the cheapest good option?

A: Zoho Expense at $3/user/month. For 5 users, that's $15/month or $180/year.

Q: Can we use free tools instead?

A: Free tools (like spreadsheets) don't prevent errors—they just record them. You'll spend more time fixing errors than the software would cost.

Q: How long does setup take?

A: Most small teams can set up and train in 2-4 hours. The time savings pay for this in the first month.

Q: What if we grow beyond 10 employees?

A: All these tools scale. Expensify and Zoho Expense work well up to 50-100 employees before you might need enterprise features.

Conclusion

For small teams under 10 employees, Expensify offers the best balance of prevention features and affordability at $5/user/month. Its policy blocking and SmartScan OCR prevent the errors that waste your time, while the simple interface works for non-finance team members.

If budget is extremely tight, Zoho Expense at $3/user/month provides good basic prevention. If you already use QuickBooks, the integrated expense tracking makes sense despite the higher cost.
