Frequently Asked Questions

What expense software works best for teams under 10 people?

Expensify, Ramp, and Zoho Expense all have strong small-team plans. Ramp is free for small teams and includes corporate cards. Expensify is best if your team travels frequently. Zoho Expense integrates best with existing Zoho or Google Workspace setups.

Do I need expense software or will a spreadsheet do?

Spreadsheets work up to about 3 people making occasional purchases. Beyond that, the time cost of manual reconciliation, lost receipts, and approval delays exceeds the software cost. Most small business expense tools start under $10/user/month.

How does expense software save money?

Through faster reimbursement cycles (reducing float), automated policy enforcement (catching out-of-policy spend before it's approved), and visibility into spending patterns that reveal wasteful recurring charges. Most teams recoup the cost within 2-3 months.

Can expense software integrate with QuickBooks?

Yes --- Expensify, Ramp, Divvy, and most major expense platforms have native QuickBooks integrations. Expenses sync automatically to the correct GL accounts, eliminating manual data entry and reducing month-end close time significantly.

What's the difference between expense software and corporate cards?

Corporate cards control spending at the point of purchase. Expense software manages reimbursements after the fact. Modern platforms like Ramp and Brex combine both --- issue virtual cards with built-in spending limits and automatic receipt matching.
