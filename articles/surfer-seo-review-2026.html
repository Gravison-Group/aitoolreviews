What Is Surfer SEO?

Surfer SEO is an AI-powered content optimization platform that analyzes the top-ranking pages for any keyword and tells you exactly what your content needs to rank: word count, keyword density, headings, images, and more. It's become the industry standard for data-driven on-page SEO.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does Surfer SEO actually improve Google rankings?

A: Independent case studies consistently show content optimized to an A or A+ Surfer score ranks higher than unoptimized content for equivalent keywords. The effect is strongest for competitive keywords where on-page optimization is a significant ranking factor.

Q: What is a good Surfer SEO content score?

A: A score of 67+ ("Good" range) is the target minimum for competitive keywords. 80+ puts you in the top tier and is consistently associated with first-page rankings for most queries.

Q: How is Surfer SEO different from Yoast SEO?

A: Yoast checks basic on-page factors (meta tags, readability, keyword density). Surfer SEO does full competitive analysis — comparing your content to the top 10 ranking pages and telling you exactly what terms to add and how much to write.

Q: Can I use Surfer SEO with AI-generated content?

A: Yes — Surfer's Content Editor integrates directly with Jasper, and you can paste AI-generated drafts into the editor for optimization. Many users generate first drafts with AI then optimize in Surfer.
