About AI Tool Reviews

Last updated: March 31, 2026

Who We Are

We're developers and AI practitioners who use these tools every day. We build production applications with LLMs, deploy AI agents that run autonomously, and ship code with AI assistants. When we review a tool, we've already been using it — often for weeks or months before writing a word.

Our Review Philosophy

The AI tool space is drowning in sponsored listicles and SEO-optimized fluff. You've seen the articles: "Top 10 AI Tools in 2026" where every tool gets 4.5 stars and the rankings mysteriously match the affiliate commission rates.

We do things differently:

  • Real usage, not demos. We test tools in actual workflows — building products, writing code, deploying to production. Not running the same "write me a haiku" prompt twenty times.
  • Honest scores. If something sucks, we say so. If a free tool beats a paid one, we'll tell you. Our reviews are not influenced by partnerships or sponsorships.
  • Developer-focused. We evaluate tools from a builder's perspective. Can you integrate this into a real application? Does it handle edge cases? What breaks at scale?
  • Updated regularly. AI tools change fast. We update our reviews when major updates ship, not just when we need fresh SEO content.

What We Cover

  • LLMs — Claude, GPT, Gemini, open-source models like Llama and Mistral. Head-to-head comparisons on real tasks.
  • Agent Platforms — OpenClaw, LangChain, AutoGPT, CrewAI, and other frameworks for building autonomous AI systems.
  • MCP Servers — Model Context Protocol servers that extend what AI agents can do in the real world.
  • Dev Tools — Coding assistants, testing tools, deployment platforms, and anything that makes developers more productive with AI.

Affiliate Disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links. This means we may earn a small commission if you sign up for a product through our link — at no additional cost to you. This helps keep the site running and the reviews free.

Important: Affiliate relationships never influence our reviews. We frequently recommend free tools over paid alternatives. If a product with an affiliate program is worse than a free option, we'll say so. Our credibility is worth more than any commission.
