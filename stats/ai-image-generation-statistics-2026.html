AI Image Generation Statistics 2026: Midjourney, DALL-E & Market Data

Key Stat: The AI image generation market hit $299 million in 2023 and is projected to reach $917 million by 2028. Over 34 million AI-generated images are created every day across all platforms, with Midjourney alone generating an estimated $200 million in annual revenue.

AI image generation has gone from an impressive party trick to a legitimate creative tool in under two years. Over 34 million images are generated each day across platforms like Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion, and Adobe Firefly — a volume that would take every photographer and illustrator on Earth years to produce. But with that scale comes fierce legal battles, professional disruption, and hard questions about authenticity. Here's the data.

34M+ AI-generated images created every day across all platforms — Everypixel Journal / Industry estimates, 2023

Market Size & Growth Projections

$299M AI image generation market value in 2023 — MarketsandMarkets, 2023
$917M projected market value by 2028 — MarketsandMarkets, 2023
  • The AI image generation market was valued at $299 million in 2023, with a projected compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of approximately 25% leading to $917 million by 2028. — MarketsandMarkets, 2023
  • The broader generative AI visual content market — including video and 3D generation — is significantly larger, estimated at $1.7 billion in 2023 with projections exceeding $10 billion by 2030. — Grand View Research, 2023
  • Approximately 34 million+ AI-generated images are created daily across all platforms, based on extrapolations from publicly available platform data. — Everypixel Journal, 2023
  • By mid-2023, the total number of AI-generated images in existence surpassed the total number of photos ever taken on Instagram. — Industry analysis, 2023
  • The text-to-image segment dominates, accounting for approximately 75% of AI image generation usage, with image-to-image (editing, inpainting) making up the remainder. — Various platform data, 2024

Midjourney Usage & Revenue

16M Midjourney users on Discord — Midjourney / Discord, 2024
$200M estimated Midjourney annual recurring revenue (2023) — Analyst estimates, 2023
  • Midjourney has approximately 16 million users on its Discord server, including free trial users and paid subscribers. — Midjourney / Discord, 2024
  • At peak usage, Midjourney generates approximately 2.5 million images per hour — roughly 700 images per second. — Midjourney / Industry estimates, 2023
  • Midjourney's estimated annual recurring revenue reached $200 million in 2023 — remarkable for a bootstrapped company with no venture capital funding and a team of fewer than 20 people. — Analyst estimates, 2023
  • Midjourney charges $10–60/month for subscriptions ranging from 200 to unlimited image generations per month. The $30/month Pro plan is the most popular tier. — Midjourney pricing, 2024
  • In early 2024, Midjourney began testing a web-based image editor, moving beyond the strictly Discord-based interface that defined its early growth. — Midjourney, 2024
  • Midjourney V6 (released late 2023) introduced significantly improved photorealism, text rendering, and style control, cementing its position as the preferred tool for professional AI artists. — Midjourney, 2023

DALL-E 3 & ChatGPT Integration

100M+ ChatGPT users with access to DALL-E 3 (ChatGPT Plus/Enterprise subscribers) — OpenAI, 2024
  • DALL-E 3 is integrated into ChatGPT Plus and Enterprise, giving over 100 million users access to image generation directly within the chat interface. — OpenAI, 2024
  • Unlike Midjourney and Stable Diffusion, DALL-E 3 excels at rendering text in images (signage, labels, documents) — a task where most other models struggle significantly. — OpenAI / Independent testing, 2023
  • DALL-E 3 introduced "conversational image generation" — users refine images through natural language rather than complex prompt engineering — making it the most accessible AI image tool for non-technical users. — OpenAI, 2023
  • OpenAI's image generation API (DALL-E 3 via the API) is priced at $0.040–0.080 per image depending on resolution, making it a common choice for commercial integration. — OpenAI pricing, 2024
  • Usage of DALL-E 3 within ChatGPT increased dramatically after OpenAI allowed all ChatGPT Plus users to enable its "GPT-4 with DALL-E" feature in late 2023. — OpenAI, 2023

Stable Diffusion & Open Source

12.5B images generated by Stable Diffusion as of mid-2023 — Stability AI, 2023
10M+ active Stable Diffusion users — Stability AI, 2023
  • Stable Diffusion had generated over 12.5 billion images as of mid-2023, according to Stability AI — more than any other single AI image platform at that time. — Stability AI, 2023
  • Stable Diffusion has 10 million+ active users, including a large community of developers who fine-tune and customize the open-source model. — Stability AI, 2023
  • As an open-source model, Stable Diffusion can be run locally on consumer-grade GPUs — a key advantage for privacy-conscious users and developers building custom applications. — Stability AI, 2022
  • Stable Diffusion 3 and SDXL Turbo (2023–2024 releases) dramatically reduced generation time to sub-second speeds on modern GPUs, enabling real-time image generation for the first time. — Stability AI, 2023–2024
  • Hundreds of thousands of community-trained Stable Diffusion checkpoints and LoRAs exist, fine-tuned for specific art styles, characters, and subjects — creating an ecosystem unlike any other AI image platform. — Civitai.com / Community estimates, 2024
  • Despite its popularity, Stability AI faced significant financial challenges in 2023–2024, including executive departures and reported difficulties raising new funding at its previous $1 billion valuation. — Bloomberg / TechCrunch, 2023–2024

Adobe Firefly & Creative Cloud

3B+ images generated by Adobe Firefly in its first year — Adobe, 2024
  • Adobe Firefly generated over 3 billion images in its first year after launch (March 2023 – March 2024). — Adobe, 2024
  • Firefly is deeply integrated into Adobe Creative Cloud, which has 30 million+ subscribers — including Photoshop, Illustrator, and Express. — Adobe, 2024
  • Adobe's key differentiator: Firefly was trained primarily on Adobe Stock images and openly licensed content, with Adobe promising to indemnify commercial users against copyright claims — a major selling point for enterprise clients. — Adobe, 2023
  • Firefly's generative fill feature in Photoshop — which allows users to select and replace areas of existing images — became one of the most-used AI features in any creative tool. — Adobe, 2024
  • Adobe introduced a pricing model where Firefly generations consume "generative credits" — usually 100–500 per month depending on the Creative Cloud plan. — Adobe pricing, 2024

Copyright Cases & Legal Landscape

3 major ongoing copyright lawsuits against AI image platforms — Court filings, 2023–2024
  • Getty Images v. Stability AI: Getty Images sued Stability AI in January 2023, alleging that Stable Diffusion was trained on 12+ million Getty watermarked images without license or compensation. As of 2026, this case is still ongoing in UK and US courts. — Getty Images / Court filings, 2023
  • Artist class action (Andersen et al. v. Stability AI et al.): A class action lawsuit filed by artists Sarah Andersen, Kelly McKernan, and Karla Ortiz against Stability AI, Midjourney, and DeviantArt, alleging copyright infringement through training on their artwork without consent. The case survived initial dismissal motions and discovery was ongoing as of 2025. — US District Court filings, 2023
  • New York Times v. OpenAI/Microsoft: The NYT sued OpenAI and Microsoft in December 2023 for copyright infringement related to the use of millions of news articles in training data. While this case primarily targets text generation, it has broad implications for all AI training data practices, including images. — NYT / Court filings, 2023
  • The US Copyright Office has ruled that AI-generated images are not copyrightable without "substantial human authorship," creating uncertainty about the commercial value of AI-generated art. — US Copyright Office, 2023
  • Shutterstock — which initially saw its stock price decline as investors feared AI disruption — partnered with OpenAI to sell DALL-E-generated images on its platform, launching a "Contributor Fund" to compensate artists whose work was used in training. — Shutterstock / OpenAI, 2023
  • Shutterstock's revenue growth slowed significantly following the AI image boom, as customers increasingly turned to AI generation tools instead of stock photography. — Shutterstock SEC filings, 2023–2024

Professional Adoption & Industry Impact

39% of graphic designers use AI image tools at least weekly — Adobe survey, 2023
$0.02–0.08 cost per AI-generated image vs $50–500 for professional stock photography — Platform pricing / Industry estimates, 2024
  • 39% of graphic designers report using AI image generation tools at least weekly, according to a 2023 Adobe survey of creative professionals. Usage is highest among designers under 35. — Adobe, 2023
  • The cost of an AI-generated image ranges from $0.02–0.08 per image on paid platforms, compared to $50–500 for a single professional stock photo or custom illustration. This price delta is the primary driver of enterprise adoption. — Platform pricing / Industry estimates, 2024
  • Stock photography market disruption: Shutterstock and Getty Images both reported significant revenue growth slowdowns in 2023 as AI image generation tools gained mainstream adoption. — SEC filings, 2023–2024
  • Independent illustrators and photographers report 20–40% decline in project inquiries since 2022, with the most significant drops in commercial illustration, product photography, and marketing visuals. — Industry surveys, 2024
  • Film and game studios increasingly use AI image generation for concept art and pre-visualization, though final production assets are still predominantly human-created. — Industry reports, 2024
  • E-commerce product photography is the most disrupted segment: AI-generated product images on model-less backgrounds save companies hundreds of dollars per product SKU. — E-commerce industry data, 2024

Detection, Fakes & Misinformation

96% of AI-generated political images shared without AI disclosure — CIGI survey, 2023
70–85% best AI image detector accuracy — far from reliable — Academic benchmarks, 2023–2024
  • 96% of AI-generated political images shared on social media are posted without any disclosure that they were AI-created, according to a 2023 CIGI/Ipsos survey. — CIGI / Ipsos, 2023
  • The best AI image detectors achieve 70–85% accuracy on benchmark datasets — meaning they fail to detect AI-generated images in 15–30% of cases and also generate false positives. No detector is close to production-ready reliable. — Academic benchmarks, 2023–2024
  • Deepfakes generated by AI image tools were involved in a notable election interference incident in the 2024 US election cycle, leading to calls for mandatory AI watermarking legislation. — News reports, 2024
  • Major platforms including TikTok, Instagram, and X (Twitter) have introduced mandatory AI labeling for certain content, though enforcement varies widely. — Platform policy announcements, 2023–2024
  • C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity) — backed by Adobe, Microsoft, OpenAI, and others — has developed an industry standard for cryptographic content credentials that trace an image's origin and edits. Adoption is growing but not yet universal. — C2PA, 2023–2024
