ChatGPT Statistics 2026: Users, Revenue & Growth

Key Stat: ChatGPT reached 200 million weekly active users by August 2024 --- doubling in less than a year and making it the fastest-growing consumer application in history. OpenAI's valuation hit $157 billion in its latest funding round.

Note: Statistics on this page are sourced from OpenAI announcements, Pew Research, Stanford AI Index, financial disclosures, and third-party analytics providers. We cite each source.

No consumer technology has grown faster than ChatGPT. When OpenAI launched it on November 30, 2022, the company expected modest interest. Instead, 1 million users signed up in just 5 days --- a record at the time. Two months later, it hit 100 million users, surpassing Instagram's growth trajectory by years. By August 2024, ChatGPT had 200 million weekly active users, and OpenAI had become one of the most valuable private companies on Earth. These statistics tell the story of the fastest tech adoption in history.

200M weekly active users as of August 2024 --- double the figure from November 2023 --- OpenAI, August 2024

Growth Milestones & Speed Records

5 days to reach 1 million users after launch (November 2022) --- OpenAI, 2022
2 months to reach 100 million users --- the fastest app ever to do so --- Reuters/UBS, 2023
  • ChatGPT launched November 30, 2022 and reached 1 million registered users in 5 days --- faster than Netflix (3.5 years), Facebook (10 months), and Spotify (5 months). --- OpenAI, 2022
  • By January 2023, ChatGPT reached 100 million monthly active users --- the fastest any consumer app has ever reached that milestone. TikTok took 9 months; Instagram took 2.5 years. --- UBS analysis, 2023
  • By November 2023, ChatGPT had 100 million weekly active users. By August 2024, that figure doubled to 200 million weekly active users. --- OpenAI, 2024
  • ChatGPT crossed 1 billion monthly visits in early 2023 --- becoming one of the fastest websites to reach that traffic threshold. --- Similarweb, 2023
  • ChatGPT's iOS app was downloaded over 1 million times in the first 6 days after its May 2023 launch. --- Apptopia, 2023

User Statistics

200M+ weekly active users (August 2024) --- OpenAI, 2024
46% of US adults have used ChatGPT --- Pew Research Center, 2023
  • 46% of US adults have tried ChatGPT at least once; that figure rises to 58% among adults aged 18-29. --- Pew Research Center, 2023
  • 15% of teenagers in the US report using ChatGPT on a daily basis. --- Pew Research Center, 2024
  • Approximately 49% of US college students have used ChatGPT to assist with coursework. --- BestColleges.com survey, 2023
  • ChatGPT is used in more than 160 countries worldwide. --- OpenAI, 2024
  • Approximately 60% of ChatGPT users access it from mobile devices; 40% from desktop. --- Similarweb estimates, 2024
  • ChatGPT Plus (paid subscription) accounts for approximately 5-8% of total active users, generating disproportionate revenue. --- Analyst estimates, 2024
  • The average ChatGPT session lasts approximately 7-8 minutes --- significantly longer than the average Google search session. --- Similarweb, 2024

Revenue & Business Model

$3.4B+ OpenAI projected annual revenue run rate in 2024 --- The Information, 2024
300% year-over-year revenue growth in 2023 --- OpenAI financial disclosures, 2024
  • OpenAI's revenue grew from approximately $28 million in 2022 to over $1.6 billion annualized in 2023 --- approximately 300% year-over-year. --- The Information / OpenAI, 2024
  • OpenAI's annual revenue run rate was projected to exceed $3.4 billion in 2024, with some estimates reaching $5 billion. --- The Information, 2024
  • ChatGPT Plus, launched February 2023, charges $20/month per user and contributed the majority of direct consumer revenue in 2023. --- OpenAI, 2023
  • OpenAI's Team plan ($25/user/month) and Enterprise plan (custom pricing) grew significantly through 2024 as businesses sought higher usage limits and privacy guarantees. --- OpenAI, 2024
  • API revenue --- from developers and businesses building on GPT --- accounts for the largest share of OpenAI's total revenue, estimated at over 60%. --- Analyst estimates, 2024
  • OpenAI processes approximately 10 trillion tokens monthly through its API as of 2024. --- Industry estimates, 2024
  • Despite massive revenue, OpenAI reported operating losses of approximately $700 million in 2023 due to extremely high compute costs --- compute remains the company's largest expense. --- The Information, 2024

OpenAI Valuation & Funding

$157B OpenAI valuation at October 2024 funding round --- OpenAI / Bloomberg, 2024
$6.6B raised in October 2024 --- largest private tech fundraise ever --- OpenAI, 2024
  • OpenAI was valued at $157 billion in its October 2024 funding round --- making it one of the most valuable private companies in history. --- OpenAI / Bloomberg, 2024
  • OpenAI raised $6.6 billion in its October 2024 round, the largest private technology fundraise in history at that time. --- OpenAI, 2024
  • Microsoft has invested over $13 billion in OpenAI across multiple rounds since 2019, making it OpenAI's largest single investor and cloud compute provider. --- Microsoft, 2023
  • OpenAI was founded in December 2015 with $1 billion in pledged funding from Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, and others. Musk departed the board in 2018. --- OpenAI, 2015/2018
  • OpenAI's valuation grew from $29 billion (January 2023) to $86 billion (early 2024) to $157 billion (October 2024) --- a 5x increase in under two years. --- Bloomberg/The Information, 2024
  • Investors in the October 2024 round included Thrive Capital, Microsoft, NVIDIA, SoftBank, and several sovereign wealth funds. --- Bloomberg, 2024

Enterprise Adoption

80%+ of Fortune 500 companies use ChatGPT through OpenAI's enterprise or API products --- OpenAI, 2024
  • OpenAI states that over 80% of Fortune 500 companies use its products --- either ChatGPT Enterprise, the API, or Microsoft Copilot (built on GPT-4). --- OpenAI, 2024
  • OpenAI had over 2 million developers building on its API as of 2024, compared to a few hundred thousand in early 2023. --- OpenAI, 2024
  • OpenAI closed over 100 new enterprise deals per week through much of 2024, according to internal estimates. --- The Information, 2024
  • ChatGPT Enterprise, launched August 2023, offers no usage caps, enterprise-grade security, and admin tools --- and became one of OpenAI's fastest-growing product lines. --- OpenAI, 2023
  • Industries with highest ChatGPT Enterprise adoption: technology, professional services, financial services, and media. --- Analyst estimates, 2024

ChatGPT Performance Benchmarks

  • GPT-4 scores in the 90th percentile on the Uniform Bar Exam --- performing better than most human law school graduates. --- OpenAI GPT-4 Technical Report, 2023
  • GPT-4 passed the US Medical Licensing Exam (USMLE) with a score well above the passing threshold, across all three steps. --- AnsibleHealth / Microsoft Research, 2023
  • GPT-4 scores in the 99th percentile on GRE Verbal Reasoning. --- OpenAI GPT-4 Technical Report, 2023
  • GPT-4 passed AP exams for Biology, Chemistry, and US History at the 4 or 5 level (equivalent to college credit). --- OpenAI GPT-4 Technical Report, 2023
  • GPT-4 achieves expert-level performance on many professional knowledge benchmarks while still making reasoning errors on novel logical problems. --- Stanford HELM benchmark, 2023
  • OpenAI's o1 reasoning model, launched September 2024, outperformed PhD students on a competitive mathematics benchmark (AIME). --- OpenAI, 2024
  • In head-to-head writing quality tests, ChatGPT-4 output is preferred over GPT-3.5 output by humans in approximately 70% of cases. --- OpenAI, 2023

Web Traffic & Usage Patterns

~1.8B monthly visits to ChatGPT.com --- Similarweb estimates, 2024
  • ChatGPT.com receives approximately 1.6-1.8 billion monthly visits, making it one of the top 10 most-visited websites in the world. --- Similarweb, 2024
  • The United States is the largest single source of ChatGPT traffic --- approximately 15-18% of all visits. --- Similarweb, 2024
  • India, Japan, Brazil, and the UK round out the top 5 countries by ChatGPT traffic. --- Similarweb, 2024
  • ChatGPT's bounce rate is low --- approximately 31% --- meaning users frequently engage in multi-message conversations. --- Similarweb estimates, 2024
  • The most common ChatGPT use cases by volume: writing assistance, coding help, research/summarization, language translation, and brainstorming. --- OpenAI usage data, 2024
  • ChatGPT processes approximately 10 million queries per day on average, with peaks during business hours in North America and Europe. --- Industry estimates, 2024

Frequently Asked Questions

How many users does ChatGPT have?

As of August 2024, ChatGPT has 200 million weekly active users --- double the 100 million it had in November 2023. It reached 1 million users in just 5 days after launch in November 2022.

How much revenue does OpenAI make?

OpenAI's revenue grew approximately 300% in 2023, reaching over $1.6 billion annualized. Revenue projections for 2024-2025 range from $3.4 billion to $5 billion annually.

What is OpenAI's valuation?

OpenAI reached a valuation of $157 billion in its October 2024 funding round --- the largest private tech fundraise in history at $6.6 billion. Microsoft has invested over $13 billion in the company.

How does ChatGPT perform on standardized tests?

GPT-4 scores in the 90th percentile on the Bar Exam, 99th percentile on GRE Verbal Reasoning, and passed the US Medical Licensing Exam. It achieves expert-level performance on many professional knowledge benchmarks.

How many companies use ChatGPT?

OpenAI reports that over 80% of Fortune 500 companies use ChatGPT. OpenAI has more than 2 million developers using its API and closed over 100 new enterprise deals per week in 2024.
