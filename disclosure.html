Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: April 2026

Our Relationship With Product Brands

AIToolReviews.co participates in affiliate marketing programs with AI software companies and the Amazon Associates Program. This means that when you click certain product links on this site and make a qualifying purchase or sign up for a paid subscription, AIToolReviews.co may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you.

The price you pay for any product is exactly the same whether you click through our link or navigate directly to the company's website. Affiliate commissions are paid by the software company or retailer, not by the consumer.

Which Links Are Affiliate Links?

We include affiliate links in tool review articles, comparison tables, and "best of" roundups. We strive to be clear when a link may result in compensation. Not all links on AIToolReviews.co are affiliate links — we frequently link to tools and resources without any affiliate relationship when they are the best reference for the topic.

How This Affects Our Editorial Recommendations

It doesn't. Our editorial process determines which AI tools are reviewed and recommended — based on real hands-on testing, feature accuracy, pricing transparency, and honest performance assessment. We do not accept payment for editorial placement or positive reviews, and affiliate relationships do not influence our ratings or recommendations.

If a tool we are affiliated with underperforms or is not the right fit for a reader's use case, we will say so clearly and recommend a better alternative, even if that alternative earns us nothing.

Amazon Associates Disclosure

AIToolReviews.co is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

FTC Compliance

This disclosure is made in accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials in advertising (16 CFR Part 255). We believe in full transparency with our readers about any financial relationships that may exist between AIToolReviews.co and the AI software companies or products mentioned on this site.

Questions?

If you have questions about our affiliate relationships or editorial policies, please visit our About page or contact us for more information.
